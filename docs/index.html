About the bot

Born into chess in 2008, when Chess.com was still small enough that Daniel Rensch hadn't yet registered. I beat him to it. Magnus once accepted my friend request — I kept the screenshot.

On the board: an obstinate 1.d4 player with white, a Caro‑Kann specialist with black, and a long-running project to dismantle the egos of overrated opponents. Peak ratings around 2300 bullet, 2150 blitz. Likes closed positions, dislikes gambits.

The bot is what happens when 22,887 of those games pass through a Maia2 neural network and pick up a Stockfish referee.

How it works

1 Opening book

A 100,000-position book built from my actual games. Frequency-gated; only positions reached in 10+ games are answered from book. Goes 30 plies deep into mainlines.

2 Maia2 policy

A 23M-parameter neural network fine-tuned on my moves. Top-1 move-prediction accuracy: 47% (up from 25% on base Maia2). The personalization signal is real.

3 Stockfish referee

Maia proposes its top 5 candidates — already in my distribution — and Stockfish at depth 10 picks the tactically soundest. Style preserved, blunders curtailed.

Who is Nick

Born into chess in 2008, when Chess.com was still small enough that Daniel Rensch hadn't yet registered. Nick beat him to it. Magnus once accepted his friend request. He kept the screenshot.

An obstinate 1.d4 player with white. A Caro-Kann specialist with black. Long-standing project to dismantle the egos of overrated opponents. Peak ratings around 2300 bullet, 2150 blitz. Likes closed positions, dislikes gambits, suspects you're outrated.

This bot is what happens when 22,887 of his most considered moves pass through a Maia2 neural network and acquire a Stockfish referee. Plays his openings with cult-like consistency; plays the rest with reasonable competence.
